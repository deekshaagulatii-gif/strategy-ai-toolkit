REQUEST FOR TENDER (fictional example for demonstration)
Strategy Development Services for Northbridge Community Health Network

Section 4: Mandatory requirements (pass/fail)
M1. Tenderers must confirm compliance with GDPR and describe how personal data collected during consultation will be stored and processed.
M2. The assignment must be completed within 16 weeks of contract signing.
M3. Tenderers must name a project lead with at least five years' experience of strategy development.

Section 5: Award criteria
Tenders that pass Section 4 will be scored as follows.
A. Understanding of the requirement (15%): show an understanding of the Network's services, context and the challenges set out in Section 2.
B. Methodology and work plan (30%): describe the approach, phases, deliverables and timeline, including how the strategy will set measurable outcomes.
C. Stakeholder engagement (15%): explain how service users, staff and partner organisations, including seldom-heard groups, will be consulted.
D. Innovation and creativity (15%): describe any innovative methods or tools that will add value beyond a standard approach.
E. Team and relevant experience (10%): outline the team, their roles and comparable assignments.
F. Price (15%): assessed from the separate price schedule.
