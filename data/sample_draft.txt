DRAFT RESPONSE (fictional example for demonstration)

1. Our understanding
Northbridge Community Health Network supports people across three counties through community nursing, mental health outreach and carer services. We understand the Network needs a strategy that responds to rising demand and shows funders the difference its services make.

2. Our approach
We propose a four-phase approach over 16 weeks: discovery (weeks 1 to 3), engagement (weeks 4 to 8), strategy drafting (weeks 9 to 13) and finalisation (weeks 14 to 16). Each phase ends with a steering group review. The strategy will set three to five outcomes, each with indicators, a data source and a baseline, so the Network can report progress every quarter.

3. Consultation
We will run six workshops with staff and an online survey for service users. We will also interview partner organisations.

4. Our team
The project will be led by our Director, who has 12 years' strategy experience in health and social care, supported by a senior consultant and an analyst. Recent assignments include corporate plans for two national health bodies.

5. Added value
We will use modern tools to make the process efficient.
